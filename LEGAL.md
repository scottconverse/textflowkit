textflowkit is a **general-purpose tool**. It can retrieve media from third-party
platforms via `yt-dlp` and related tooling.

- Compliance with **copyright law** for any media you process is **your
  responsibility**.
- Compliance with each platform's **terms of service** is **your responsibility**.
- **Privacy and consent** obligations for recordings of other people are **your
  responsibility**. Recording-consent law varies by jurisdiction.
- The maintainers do not host, store, or distribute third-party media.

The project exists to transcribe media **you are entitled to process** — your own
recordings, public-domain works, openly licensed material, or content you have
permission to use.
